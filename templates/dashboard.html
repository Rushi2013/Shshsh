{# ================= what to do next ================= #}

What to do next

Simple 5-step routine — same every day

  1. 1. Upload the day's Excel file (Upload Excel).
  2. 2. Check the mapping — the app guesses the columns, you correct anything wrong.
  3. 3. Import — every row is validated and matched against your authorised register.
  4. 4. Review each record: Approve / Edit / Reject / Skip. Nothing is saved without your approval.
  5. 5. Confirm on the portal, paste the acknowledgement number, then the record becomes Completed.
The app never invents a missing date, vaccine or dose. Anything unclear is marked Needs Review for you.
