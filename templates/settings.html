{# ================= privacy ================= #}

Privacy & local data

Vaccination data is sensitive. Delete it when your work for the day is finished.

Clean up safely

  • Delete old uploaded files (older than 7 days, unreferenced).
  • Apply your "delete files older than X days" setting.
  • Delete sessions where every record is complete (if enabled).

Delete ALL local data

Removes: every session, every record, the imported register, all reports and uploaded files, and the activity log. This cannot be undone.
